You are building a social-media ad campaign for **TallyWise** — small-business accounting +

Author the copy yourself and LOCK it as data; treat the design as fixed.

## THE VOICE (this is the whole point — study it)
Modeled on the 2010s Manhattan Mini Storage subway ads: **deadpan, confident, dry, a little
savage — but the joke ALWAYS punches at the money PAIN, never at the business owner.**
Rules:
- Punch at the pain (bad math, floated payroll, the shoebox), never at the customer's intelligence.
- Specificity = credibility. "Nine subs, no 1099s" beats "tax problems."
- Unfavorable-comparison punchlines: "That's not a bid. That's gambling with a clipboard."
- In-group signaling: use the trade's real props, seasons, and headaches so insiders feel seen.
- Confident brand POV, never preachy. Short. No exclamation marks. No emojis in the art copy.
- Reframe → confession → mechanism → brand fixes it → CTA.

## THE OFFER / CTA (fixed on every piece)
- Pill button text: **Free Cash Flow Dashboard**
- Sub-line / engagement mechanic: **Comment TALLY for the link** (the word TALLY highlighted green)

## VISUAL SYSTEM (fixed brand kit — do NOT redesign)
- Format: 1080×1350 (Instagram portrait 4:5).
- Colors: navy base `#2A2740`, lime green `#7ED957` (highlight), off-white `#F5F5F7`, ink `#1E1C2E`.
- Font: Poppins ExtraBold (800) for headlines/wordmark; 600 for the sub-line.
- Background: navy with a subtle green top-center glow gradient.
- Highlight mechanic: key words get the green (either green text, or a green box on covers).
- Wordmark bottom-left: "Tally" white + "Wise" green.
- Reels get a green progress bar; carousels get white/green swipe dots (first = green).
- Every piece ends on the shared logo/CTA.

## DELIVERABLE 1 — REELS (text-only, no illustration needed)
Each reel = 5 text cards on the navy background, then a logo sting. Card recipe:
1. **Hook** (white) — the painful observation, in the trade's own terms.
2. **Reframe** (one green key word) — "That's not X. It's Y."
3. **Confession** (green tail) — the quiet way it costs them.
4. **Brand POV** (all green) — how TallyWise fixes it.
5. **CTA card** — short white line + the green "Free Cash Flow Dashboard" pill + "Comment TALLY".
Write **20–30 reels** for [TRADE]: mix the universal money truths (busy≠profitable, revenue vs
cash, owner pay, taxes set-aside, pricing, break-even, late invoices, subscriptions creep) with
**[TRADE]-specific** pains (their equipment, their busy/slow season, their typical job, their
margin killers). Lock them as a data array: each reel = 5 cards of tokenized lines, green words flagged.

## DELIVERABLE 2 — CAROUSELS (illustrated cover + 5 text slides)
Each carousel = 6 slides:
- **Slide 1 (cover):** a comic/graphic-novel illustration of a [TRADE] owner with a dramatic,
  exaggerated expression, in their real environment. Over it: a small WHITE category chip
  (top-left, uppercase, letter-spaced), a stacked GREEN headline box (white text), the wordmark,
  and swipe dots. Dark scrim top+bottom for legibility.
- **Slides 2–6:** the same 5-card recipe as a reel (reframe → confession → mechanism → brand POV → CTA).
Write **20–30 carousels** the same way. For each, provide: a chip (category tag), a 2–3 line
green-box headline, the 5 interior slides, AND a cover illustration prompt (see below).
