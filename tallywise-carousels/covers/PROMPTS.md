# Tallywise Carousel Covers — 30 Higgsfield prompts

**How to use:** generate each prompt, pick the best of the 4 variations, save it as the
listed **filename** into `tallywise-carousels/public/covers/`. I composite the chip +
green headline + wordmark + dots on top and render the full carousel — so **leave the top
third dark and clutter-free, and put NO text/lettering in the image.**

Every prompt already ends with the shared **STYLE BLOCK** so all 30 covers match your
reference set (comic-book, moody navy/teal, green rim light, dramatic expression, 4:5).
Different character + expression each time, per your ask.

**Aspect ratio:** 4:5 portrait (1080×1350). If Higgsfield only offers 9:16, generate 9:16
and I'll crop to 4:5 — just keep the subject centered.

---

### STYLE BLOCK (already appended to every prompt below)
> — comic-book graphic-novel illustration, heavy black ink outlines, dramatic cinematic
> lighting, moody dark navy-and-teal color palette with a subtle lime-green rim light,
> expressive exaggerated facial expression, realistic cluttered props, vertical 4:5
> portrait framing, the TOP THIRD of the image noticeably darker and free of clutter for
> headline text, absolutely NO text, letters, numbers or logos anywhere in the image.
