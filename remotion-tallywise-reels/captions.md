## 02 · Money in the bank isn't profit.

**Your bank balance is lying to you. 💸**

$284,000 in the account. Feels like a great month, right?

Now subtract what that cash is already promised to:
→ Sales & payroll tax you already owe: −$61,000
→ Payroll hitting Friday: −$88,000
→ Vendor invoices due this month: −$96,000

Actually yours? **$39,000.**

Same balance. Wildly different truth. This is how good businesses go broke *quietly* — the owner reads the balance as profit, spends against it, and gets blindsided when the real bills clear.

Money in the bank isn't profit. It's money that hasn't left yet.
